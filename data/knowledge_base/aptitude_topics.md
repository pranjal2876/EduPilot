### 1. Profit and Loss
- **Cost Price (CP)**: The total amount invested to purchase or manufacture an item, including overhead costs.
- **Selling Price (SP)**: The price at which the article is sold to a customer.
- **Marked Price (MP / List Price)**: The price labeled on the article before applying discounts.
- **Formulas**:
  - Profit ($P$) $= SP - CP$ (when $SP > CP$)
  - Loss ($L$) $= CP - SP$ (when $CP > SP$)
  - Profit Percentage: $\text{Profit \%} = \frac{SP - CP}{CP} \times 100$
  - Loss Percentage: $\text{Loss \%} = \frac{CP - SP}{CP} \times 100$
  - Note: Profit % and Loss % are ALWAYS calculated with respect to Cost Price ($CP$) unless explicitly stated otherwise.
  - Selling Price with Profit: $SP = CP \times \left(1 + \frac{\text{Profit \%}}{100}\right)$
  - Selling Price with Loss: $SP = CP \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{Loss \%}}{100}\right)$
- **Discounts**:
  - $\text{Discount} = MP - SP$
  - $\text{Discount \%} = \frac{MP - SP}{MP} \times 100$
  - Two successive discounts of $d_1\%$ and $d_2\%$ are equivalent to a single net discount of:
    $$\text{Net Discount} = d_1 + d_2 - \frac{d_1 \times d_2}{100}\%$$
- **Dishonest Merchant Problem**:
  If a shopkeeper claims to sell goods at cost price but uses a false weight of $W_{\text{false}}$ instead of true weight $W_{\text{true}}$:
  $$\text{Gain \%} = \frac{W_{\text{true}} - W_{\text{false}}}{W_{\text{false}}} \times 100$$

### 2. Percentages
- A percentage represents a fraction with denominator 100 ($x\% = \frac{x}{100}$).
- **Percentage Change**:
  $$\text{Percentage Change} = \frac{\text{Final Value} - \text{Initial Value}}{\text{Initial Value}} \times 100$$
- **Successive Percentage Changes**:
  If a quantity is increased by $a\%$ and then altered by $b\%$, the net effective change is:
  $$\text{Net Change \%} = a + b + \frac{a \times b}{100}\%$$
- **Price and Consumption Invariance**:
  If the price of a commodity increases by $R\%$, the percentage reduction in consumption required to keep expenditure constant is:
  $$\text{Reduction \%} = \frac{R}{100 + R} \times 100$$

### 3. Time and Work
- **Reciprocal Work Principle**: If a person can complete a work in $N$ days, the fraction of work done in 1 day is $\frac{1}{N}$.
- **Combined Work**:
  If Person A takes $A$ days and Person B takes $B$ days to complete a work independently:
  $$\text{Rate of (A + B)} = \frac{1}{A} + \frac{1}{B} = \frac{A + B}{A \times B}$$
  $$\text{Time taken together} = \frac{A \times B}{A + B} \text{ days}$$
- **Efficiency Relationship**:
  $$\text{Total Work} = \text{Efficiency} \times \text{Time}$$
  If Person A is twice as efficient as Person B, Person A takes half the time taken by Person B.
- **Pipes and Cisterns**:
  An inlet pipe fills at rate $+\frac{1}{T_{\text{in}}}$, while an outlet (leak) empties at rate $-\frac{1}{T_{\text{out}}}$. Net filling rate $= \frac{1}{T_{\text{in}}} - \frac{1}{T_{\text{out}}}$.

### 4. Number Systems & Divisibility
- **Divisibility Rules**:
  - By 2: Last digit is even.
  - By 3: Sum of all digits is divisible by 3.
  - By 4: Number formed by the last two digits is divisible by 4.
  - By 8: Number formed by the last three digits is divisible by 8.
  - By 9: Sum of all digits is divisible by 9.
  - By 11: Difference between sum of digits at odd places and sum of digits at even places is 0 or a multiple of 11.
- **Unit Digits & Cyclicity**:
  Digits repeat their unit places in cycles of powers:
  - 2, 3, 7, 8 have cyclicity 4.
  - 4, 9 have cyclicity 2.
  - 0, 1, 5, 6 have cyclicity 1 (unit digit never changes with positive integer powers).

### 5. HCF (Highest Common Factor) & LCM (Least Common Multiple)
- **HCF (GCD)**: The greatest integer that divides each given number without a remainder.
- **LCM**: The smallest positive integer that is divisible by all the given numbers.
- **Fundamental Theorem for Two Numbers**:
  For any two positive integers $A$ and $B$:
  $$\text{HCF}(A, B) \times \text{LCM}(A, B) = A \times B$$
- **Fractions**:
  $$\text{HCF of Fractions} = \frac{\text{HCF of Numerators}}{\text{LCM of Denominators}}$$
  $$\text{LCM of Fractions} = \frac{\text{LCM of Numerators}}{\text{HCF of Denominators}}$$

### 6. Alligation and Mixture
- **Rule of Alligation**: Enables finding the ratio in which two ingredients at given prices (cheaper $c$ and dearer $d$) must be mixed to produce a mixture at a mean price $m$.
  $$\frac{\text{Quantity of Cheaper}}{\text{Quantity of Dearer}} = \frac{d - m}{m - c}$$
- **Repeated Dilution / Replacement**:
  If a vessel contains $a$ units of pure liquid and $b$ units are drawn off and replaced with water $n$ successive times:
  $$\text{Quantity of Pure Liquid Remaining} = a \times \left(1 - \frac{b}{a}\right)^n$$

### 7. Simple and Compound Interest
- **Simple Interest (SI)**:
  $$\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$
- **Compound Interest (CI)**:
  $$A = P \times \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^n$$
  $$\text{CI} = A - P$$
  For compounding half-yearly, rate becomes $R/2$ and periods become $2n$.
